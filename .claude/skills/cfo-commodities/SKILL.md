# Commodities

## Gold (core diversifier, 5-12% of portfolio)
Bullish: falling real yields, Fed cuts, weak USD, central-bank buying, geopolitical stress,
high inflation, Indian festive/wedding demand (Dhanteras/Diwali, Akshaya Tritiya).
Bearish: rising real yields / Fed hikes, strong USD, risk-on euphoria, ETF outflows.
Rule: accumulate in tranches after a 5-10% pullback from the high; rebalance when it exceeds
target by 3+ points. For INR investors gold also hedges rupee depreciation.

## Silver (satellite, ≤3%)
Half monetary, half industrial (solar, electronics). ~1.5-2x gold's volatility.
Watch the gold/silver ratio: >85 silver is cheap vs gold, <65 rich. Buy only on deep dips.

## Oil (signal, not a holding)
Brent > $90 hurts India (current account, INR, inflation, OMC margins) and helps upstream
(ONGC, Oil India); Reliance is mixed (refining margins vs input cost). Oil spikes from supply
shocks (Hormuz, sanctions) tend to mean-revert; demand-led rises persist.
Feed conclusions into `cfo-news-policy` judgements under `gold`, `silver`, `india_equity`.
