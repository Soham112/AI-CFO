# Stock deep dive

1. `python -m dicfo stock <TICKER>` (NSE tickers end in `.NS`). If blocked, gather the same
   fields from the company's latest results and a stats page via web search.
2. **Business:** what earns the money, segment mix, moat (network effects, switching costs,
   brand, cost advantage, licences). For Reliance: O2C/refining margins (GRM), Jio ARPU, retail
   growth, new-energy capex. For Google: Search share, YouTube, Cloud growth/margin, capex,
   antitrust remedies, AI competition.
3. **Numbers:** revenue & EPS growth (3y), ROE/ROCE, operating margin trend, debt, FCF, buybacks.
4. **Valuation:** forward P/E vs its own 5y range and vs peers; PEG; earnings yield vs the
   10y bond; reverse-DCF sanity (what growth does the price imply?).
5. **Chart:** price vs 50/200-DMA, RSI, distance from 52w high, support zones. Charts set the
   *timing* of staged buys, never the *decision*.
6. **Catalysts & risks (next 12m):** earnings dates, regulation, policy (tariffs, Trump actions,
   Indian govt), oil price sensitivity, INR.
7. **Lenses:** report the Buffett/Graham/Lynch/India-growth/Damani checks from the tool output.
8. **Verdict:** ACCUMULATE (staged) / HOLD-SIP / WATCH / AVOID, with: fair-value range, buy
   zones (e.g. 1/3 now, 1/3 at -8%, 1/3 at -15%), position size cap (≤5% of portfolio per stock),
   and what would make you wrong.
