# Investor lenses

Philosophies encoded as checks (see `dicfo/analysis/fundamentals.py`). Use them to sanity-check
the model, not to imitate anyone.

| Lens | Core idea | What it says in a selloff |
|---|---|---|
| Warren Buffett | Wonderful business, fair price, moat, high ROE, low debt, hold forever. "Be greedy when others are fearful." | Buy quality you understand on fear; ignore macro forecasts; keep cash for opportunities. |
| Benjamin Graham | Margin of safety, Graham number, Mr. Market's moods. | Only buy well below intrinsic value; diversify. |
| Peter Lynch | Growth at a reasonable price (PEG < 1), invest in what you know. | Corrections are normal; own businesses not tickers. |
| Ray Dalio | All-weather: balance assets across growth/inflation regimes; gold as a core diversifier. | Rebalance into what fell; hold gold against currency debasement. |
| Rakesh Jhunjhunwala | Long-term India growth bull, conviction in quality compounders. | India dips are buying opportunities for the patient. |
| Radhakishan Damani | Quiet value in simple consumer/retail businesses, clean balance sheets. | Buy great consumer franchises when they're out of favour. |
| Nikhil Kamath | India consumption, demographics, financialisation; index-first for most people. | Don't time; SIP through it; small own-conviction bets. |
| Akshat Shrivastava | Macro-aware asset allocation, geographic diversification (India + US), gold/real assets as hedge, keep cash to buy fear, watch INR depreciation. | Rebalance between US and India by valuation; accumulate in tranches. |

Note: the user mentioned "Ruchan Adani"; we assumed Radhakishan Damani. Confirm with the user.
